# Business Playbook

Operating notes the agents can query when running workflows.

## Content pipeline
1. Ideate topics from audience questions and analytics (check the Metrics tab for
   which recent videos over- or under-performed).
2. Script with the knowledge base as source material.
3. Publish, then log a workflow item to track promotion tasks.

## Customer handling
- New leads get a reply within one business day.
- Premium community members get priority; their requests become `active` workflows.
- Escalate anything involving refunds or account access to a human.

## Weekly cadence
- Monday: review metrics, pick the week's content focus.
- Wednesday: community call prep.
- Friday: close out `done` workflows and archive learnings into this knowledge base.
